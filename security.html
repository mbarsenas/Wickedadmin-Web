Security

WickedAdmin applies security-first engineering practices to its company website and products.

Company website

  • HTTPS is required for public traffic.
  • The public website does not require customer accounts.
  • Access to hosting and source-control systems is restricted.
  • Operational health and recovery procedures are reviewed regularly.

Independent product boundaries

AgentLedger, TenantIQ365, and CommentHarbor operate as separate products with their own authentication, data-handling, billing, and security documentation. A company-site inquiry does not grant access to any product account or customer data.

Responsible reporting

If you believe you found a security issue, do not include secrets, credentials, payment data, or sensitive customer information in your initial message. Contact WickedAdmin using the homepage contact link with the subject “Security report,” and we will coordinate a safe reporting channel.
